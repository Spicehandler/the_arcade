The Story

Background

Growing up in the 90's and early 2000's means I was around for the boom in home internet access, cyber-cafe culture, and the evolution of video games from the 8-bit to 16-bit era. I also lived through the evolution of home computing from the DOS era, my first family PC, to Windows 3.1 and later to 98. I was priveleged to experience dialing up to AOL for the first time, downloading my first mp3 from Napster, and later playing online games on sites like Shockwave and Candystand. The spirit of these sites is what I would love to bring to this page here. I was always excited by tech but never learned what was going on behind the curtain. Which is way now I am pursuing my MSCS degree.
